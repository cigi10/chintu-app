# CHINTU — चिंटू
## The Study Companion App for Indian Students
### Product & Pitch Document · June 2026

---

## THE ONE-LINE PITCH

Chintu is a gamified study planner for JEE, NEET, and placement students — with a living companion character, real-time study rooms, and a timetable that actually fits how Indian students study. No AI mentoring. No emotional advice. Just a tiny friend who helps you show up.

---

## WHY THIS NAME, WHY NOW

Every other study app treats students like productivity machines.

Chintu treats them like kids — because that's what they are. A 17-year-old preparing for JEE is a child carrying an adult's pressure. They don't need a coach. They don't need an AI therapist. They need a small, silly, loveable companion who just sits with them while they study, cheers when they finish a chapter, and looks sleepy when it's 1am.

Chintu. That's the character. That's the brand. That's the whole emotional thesis in one name.

---

## THE PROBLEM IN THREE SENTENCES

Indian students preparing for JEE, NEET, and placements study in complete emotional isolation. Every existing app either punishes them for missing days or gives them tools built for Western students with no cultural context. Nobody has built a study companion that feels like it belongs to them.

---

## WHAT CHINTU IS — EXACTLY

Chintu is four things combined into one app that currently don't exist together anywhere:

**1. A living companion character** (like Study Bunny, but Indian, named, and yours)
**2. A visual school-style timetable** (like Study Bunny's weekly view)
**3. Live study rooms where you can see others studying** (like Flip's Study With Me)
**4. A reward and customisation system** (coins → dress up Chintu, upgrade his room)

That's it. No AI that talks to you about your feelings. No mentoring. No journaling prompts that ask how you feel about your mother. A study planner with a soul.

---

## THE COMPANION — HOW CHINTU ACTUALLY WORKS

This is the most important section. The AI question is answered here permanently.

**Chintu communicates through:**
- Expressions and animations (happy, sleepy, proud, bored, excited)
- Short practical check-ins only:
  - "How much of Chapter 4 did you finish?"
  - "Need a break or keep going?"
  - "You've been at it 45 minutes — short break?"
  - "Thermodynamics is due for revision. Today or tomorrow?"
- Celebration reactions when you complete sessions or chapters

**Chintu never:**
- Asks how you feel emotionally
- Gives advice about stress or mental health
- Tries to be your therapist or mentor
- Says things that could be misinterpreted in a dark moment

**Why this is the right call:** AI cannot replace human emotion. When it tries to, it gets it wrong, and the consequences in a student population with real mental health pressure are too serious to risk. Chintu's warmth lives entirely in his design, his animations, and his reactions — not in words generated by a language model.

**The AI layer is minimal and purely practical.** It helps with:
- Scheduling (take the syllabus + exam date → generate a study plan)
- Portion tracking (mark topics done, suggest what's due next)
- Simple check-ins (multiple choice, not open-ended)

Nothing more. This is not a limitation. This is the product's most honest and defensible design decision.

---

## THE THREE EXAM MODES

Students choose at signup. Everything adapts to their choice.

**Mode 1: JEE (Mains + Advanced)**
Predefined syllabus loaded automatically. Physics, Chemistry, Maths — all topics with standard weightage. Exam dates set by student (unlimited — no arbitrary 4-goal cap). Timetable generated around their exam date.

**Mode 2: NEET**
Same structure. Biology, Physics, Chemistry. Official NTA syllabus built in. Students mark chapters done, Chintu tracks what's overdue for revision.

**Mode 3: Placements / Masters**
No predefined syllabus — the student builds their own. "Add topics you're studying" — DSA, System Design, GRE Vocab, Aptitude, whatever. Chintu tracks exactly what they input. This is more flexible and more honest than pretending we know every company's interview syllabus.

**Future modes:** GATE (defined syllabus, easy to add), UPSC (defined syllabus, Phase 2).

## THE FEATURES — ONE BY ONE

### Timetable (from Study Bunny)
- Visual school-style weekly grid
- You assign subjects to time slots
- Chintu's face appears in each slot — looking eager before, satisfied after
- Colour-coded by subject
- Drag and drop to rearrange
- Unlimited exam dates — set as many as you have
- Unlimited goals — no four-goal cap, ever

### Study Timer
- Pomodoro or custom timer
- Chintu studies alongside you during the session (animation)
- Gets sleepy if you go past midnight
- Celebrates when session ends
- Logs all sessions automatically

### Study Rooms — The Flip Feature (this is important)
- Live rooms where you can see how many people are currently studying
- Anonymous — you see "47 students online right now"
- Optional: themed rooms (JEE Physics, NEET Bio, Placement DSA)
- No chat. No socialising. Just the ambient feeling of not being alone
- This is the feature that makes students feel less isolated without any of the liability of actual social interaction

### Coins and Chintu Customisation (the Study Bunny reward loop)
- Earn coins by completing study sessions, finishing chapters, maintaining streaks
- Spend coins on: Chintu outfits, Chintu's room furniture, room wallpapers, desk items
- Seasonal drops — new items for exam season, festivals, summer
- This is not frivolous — this is the retention engine. Students come back to study because they want to buy Chintu new glasses

### Portion Tracker
- Full syllabus loaded for JEE/NEET
- Mark topics as: Not Started / In Progress / Done / Needs Revision
- Chintu's face on the dashboard reflects your overall progress (worried if you're behind, calm if on track)
- Revision scheduler built in — marks topics that are overdue based on spaced repetition logic

### Journal (lightweight)
- One daily text box: "What did I do today?"
- No prompts. No guided reflection. No emotional questions.
- Just: what did you study, optional notes
- Stays private. Never shared. Never analysed by AI.
- This is a log, not therapy

### Guilt-Free Reset
- Miss days? Chintu doesn't punish you
- Come back and he just says: "You're back. Want to start with something small?"
- Streak resets quietly, no dramatic broken counter
- After 3+ resets with zero sessions: Chintu gently asks "Hey, what's going on?" — one question, no lecture
- Students who return after a break are treated exactly like students who never left

## THE MENTAL HEALTH BOUNDARY — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Persistent footer on every screen: iCall helpline — 9152987821 — free, confidential, for students.

This is not a legal disclaimer. It is a genuine commitment.

Chintu will never try to counsel a student. If a student types something that contains clear distress signals in any text input, the response is a single pre-written message: "This sounds hard. Please talk to someone who can actually help — iCall: 9152987821, free and confidential." Then silence. No AI response. No generated empathy. A human-written sentence pointing to a real human.

## APP VS WEBSITE — THE DECISION

**Build as a web app first. Build Android second. Never build iOS first.**

Web app first because: zero app store approval wait, deploy in hours, easier to iterate, students can use it on laptops during study sessions.

Android second because: 90%+ of Indian students are on Android phones, push notifications are essential for habit formation ("Chintu misses you — 3 days since your last session"), and the coin/reward loop feels more natural on mobile.

iOS last or never in early stage — the 30% App Store cut on every subscription hurts more when margins matter, and the Indian student demographic is overwhelmingly Android.

---

## THE TECHNICAL REALITY

**Web app:** React frontend, Node.js or Next.js backend, Supabase for database, Vercel for hosting. Total monthly cost at 1,000 users: under ₹2,000.

**AI usage:** Minimal by design. The scheduling algorithm (exam date + syllabus → daily plan) is rule-based logic, not AI. The check-in system is multiple choice, not language model. The only AI call is the initial plan generation — once per student, not per session. Cost per user: close to ₹0 per month after setup.

**Study Rooms:** Realtime presence using Supabase Realtime or a simple WebSocket counter. Shows number of users currently in a session. No video, no audio, no chat — just a number. Technically trivial. Emotionally significant.

**Character animations:** MVP uses CSS/SVG animations — costs ₹0 to build. Revenue funds proper illustrated character version. Chintu starts simple and gets better as the product is validated.

## WHAT THIS IS NOT

Not a tutoring app. Not a doubt-solving service. Not an AI mentor. Not a mental health app. Not a social network. Not a content platform.

Chintu is a study planner with a companion. That's it. The magic is in doing that one thing with more care, more cultural understanding, and more warmth than anyone has done it before.

## THE ROADMAP — HONEST VERSION

**Weeks 1–6: Build the web MVP**
Timetable. Timer. Portion tracker (JEE + NEET only). CSS Chintu character. Basic coins. No study rooms yet. No journal yet. Deploy and share.

**Month 2–3: Get the first 500 real users**
Share as a student who built something for themselves, not as a startup launching a product. Post on Twitter, r/JEENEETPrep, relevant Telegram groups. Interview every person who signs up. Fix everything they complain about.

**Gate 1 — Month 6:** 1,000 users, 30%+ Day-7 retention. If not met — fix the product, not the marketing.

**Month 4–6: Add the social layer**
Study Rooms (realtime presence counter). Journal. Guilt-free reset logic. Coin shop with first items.

**Month 7–9: Android app**
Only after web is validated. Push notifications change the retention curve. This is when habit formation gets serious.

**Month 10–18: Growth**
Placement mode. GATE mode. Coaching centre partnerships. Creator outreach. SEO content targeting "JEE study schedule" and "how to restart studying after a break." First paid plan launched only after free users prove they love it.

---

## THE BUSINESS MODEL — KEPT SIMPLE

**Free forever:** Timetable, timer, portion tracker, study rooms, basic Chintu, 3 coin items.

**Premium (₹99–₹149/month):** Unlimited coin earning, full Chintu shop, advanced analytics, unlimited exam dates (though this should honestly be free too), seasonal exclusive items.

**The real monetisation engine is the coin shop.** Students don't pay for features — they pay to dress up Chintu. This is proven. Study Bunny, Duolingo, every gamified app — cosmetics convert better than features because the emotional attachment is to the character, not the tools.

Year 1 goal: Not ₹1 crore. 1,000 students who genuinely love Chintu. Revenue follows love.
