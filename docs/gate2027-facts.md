## 5. Normalization and GATE score (GATE 2027, IB section 8.1, pp. 27–28)

- Raw marks are used for single-session papers; multi-session papers use **normalized marks**.
- Assumption stated in the IB: the distribution of candidates' abilities is the same across sessions.
- Normalized marks of candidate j in session i:

  M̂ij = [(M̄tg − Mqg) / (M̄ti − Miq)] × (Mij − Miq) + Mqg

  where Mij = actual marks; M̄tg = average of the top 0.1% across all sessions; Mqg = mean + standard
  deviation across all sessions; M̄ti = average of the top 0.1% in session i; Miq = mean + standard deviation
  of session i.
- GATE score:

  GATE Score = Sq + (St − Sq) × (M − Mq) / (M̄t − Mq)

  where M = marks (normalized for multi-session papers); Mq = General-category qualifying marks;
  M̄t = mean of the top 0.1% or top 10 (whichever is larger) of candidates in the paper; **Sq = 350**,
  **St = 900**.
- Qualifying marks (General) = max(25, min(40, μ + σ)), μ and σ being the mean and standard deviation of
  all candidates in the paper. OBC-NCL/EWS = 9/10 of General; SC/ST/PwD = 2/3 of General.

## 6. Score validity and scorecards (GATE 2027)

- The GATE 2027 score is valid for **three years** from the date results are announced (IB key-information summary, section 5 and section 8.2).
- Scorecards are downloadable by qualified candidates; after the free download period, ₹500 per paper.
  From 1 January 2028, scorecards will not be issued for GATE 2027. No hard copies. (IB section 8.2)
- Admission based on the GATE score is decided by each admitting institute, not the Organizing Institute
  (FAQs).

## 7. Eligibility (GATE 2027)

From the Eligibility page and the IB summary:

- Currently in the **3rd or higher year** of any undergraduate degree programme, or having completed any
  government-approved degree in Engineering/Technology/Architecture/Science/Commerce/Arts/Humanities.
- Currently in the 5th/6th/7th or higher semester of, or having completed, M.B.B.S./B.D.S./B.V.Sc./
  B.Sc. (Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry), is also eligible (IB).
- Degrees from outside India: currently in the 3rd or higher year of, or having completed, a bachelor's
  degree of at least three years in those fields.
- Professional society certifications (IE, ICE, IETE, AeSI, IIChE, IIM, IIIE) count only if approved by
  MoE/AICTE/UGC/UPSC as equivalent to B.E./B.Tech./B.Arch./B.Planning.
- Candidates pursuing or holding a higher degree than those in the eligibility table may appear.
- **No age limit.** (IB)
- The full degree-by-degree table is on the Eligibility page; quote it by linking, not paraphrasing.

## 8. Application process (GATE 2027)

- Applications only through GOAPS, reached from the official website. (IB)
- Live facial details are captured during registration. (IB)
- Registration through **DigiLocker is mandatory for all Indian nationals** (homepage notice). The IB
  recommends a verified DigiLocker account; with an unverified account GATE may ask for more documents, and
  originals must be shown at the exam venue.
- Candidates must not delete the DigiLocker account used to apply; later communication refers to it
  (homepage notice).
- Fees are paid electronically (UPI, debit/credit card, wallet, internet banking). (IB)
